🧭 How to See These Names in MongoDB Compass:

  1. Open MongoDB Compass and connect to your database
  2. Click on "resumebuilder" database (or your database name)
  3. Click on "resumes" collection
  4. Look for the "header.name" field in each document
  5. If you don't see names, click "Refresh" button in Compass
  6. Filter out defaults: Use filter {"header.name": {"$ne": "Your Name"}}

💡 Tip: The names should appear in the "header.name" field of each resume document!
